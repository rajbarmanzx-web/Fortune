Zero-Knowledge Proof: Cryptographic Architecture

VERICRED uses the Fiat-Shamir Non-Interactive Zero-Knowledge (NIZK) Range Proof protocol to prove statements like "CGPA ≥ 8.0" without revealing actual grades:

1. Prover Private Commitment
C = Keccak256(CGPA: 8.70 || Salt: r)
Kept inside student local wallet. Computationally irreversible.
2. Range Constraint Delta
Δ = CGPA (8.70) - Target Threshold (8.00) = +0.70 ≥ 0
Proof verifies Δ is non-negative without revealing Δ or CGPA.
3. Fiat-Shamir Cryptographic Challenge
e = SHA256(C || TargetThreshold || EphemeralNonce)
Transforms interactive ZKP into non-interactive proof; prevents replay attacks.
